How to Compress Image to 20KB, 50KB, or 100KB for Government Exams

Many Indian government exam applications require you to upload a passport-size photograph within a specific file size limit. Common requirements include:

  • Compress image to 20 KB — Required by some state police and recruitment exams
  • Compress image to 50 KB — Common for SSC CGL, CHSL, and many banking exams
  • Compress image under 100 KB — Required by UPSC and several central government exams
